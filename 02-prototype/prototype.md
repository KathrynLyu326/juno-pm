## What it demonstrates

**Assumption:** Employees with busy schedules would benefit from an AI assistant that proactively identifies everyday needs and coordinates colleagues to help fulfill them.

My prototype demonstrates how an AI Office Concierge, embedded in the Webex Agentic App, can detect when an employee might miss lunch due to back-to-back meetings, recommend a personalized meal, and coordinate with a colleague who can pick it up. It tests whether users find proactive, AI-coordinated workplace errands valuable, convenient, and trustworthy.

_____

## Debrief

**What worked:** The prototype makes the concept tangible by showing an end-to-end workflow, from proactive lunch recommendations to ordering, colleague matching, and delivery confirmation. Integrating it into Webex's existing Needs Attention experience makes the interaction feel natural rather than requiring another standalone app.

**What felt like a toy:**
1. Our company cafeteria currently doesn't support online menus or ordering, making the AI-powered ordering experience difficult to implement in reality.
2. It's unclear whether offering monetary incentives for colleagues to run errands complies with company policies.

**What I'd change next:** I'd explore integration options with cafeteria services or alternative ordering workflows, and validate company policies around peer-to-peer errands and incentives. I'd also test whether employees would voluntarily help colleagues without monetary rewards and how reliably the matching system could work in practice.
